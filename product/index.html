One screen for every price that is behind.

A live board, a history and a weekly Ledger, built around your brands, your markets and your desk. Alerts can follow wherever your traders look.

Live board

What is wrong right now, first.

One row per selection, with your brands side by side against the fair price. Open windows rise to the top, with how long they have been open.

Your brands side by side

One row per selection with every brand's price on it, so you see at once which brands are behind.

Status in plain words

Open for 54 minutes, closed by your price, still open at kick-off. No codes to learn.

The price over time

The selected row shows your price against the fair price over the last hours, and the moment it fell behind.

Alerts

The trader on shift could hear about it while it is still open.

Sulku can send each window as one sentence: the match, your price, the fair price and how long it has been behind. A second one would follow when the price is fixed.

It could go to Slack or Teams, or as a feed into your own trading tools, with alert levels set with your traders per sport and price band. Today, open windows show on the live board.

History

Every window, and what closed it.

Each window is kept with its prices, how long it stayed open and what closed it: your own price change, the market coming back, or kick-off.

Every line has its times, so your traders can check it against their own logs.

Ledger

What slow prices cost, every week.

One page for the head of trading: how many windows opened, where they came from by sport, league, hour and brand, how long they stayed open, and how much of it your desk closed.

With your stake data, the Ledger can turn the edge given away into the money it cost.

Data and deployment

Start from the outside. Move inside when you want speed.

Sulku needs nothing from your systems to start. Your own price feed makes it faster and opens in-play. All services

Your published prices

We read the prices your customers see, per brand and per market, and compare them with the fair price every minute.

Your price feed

On your own price stream, Sulku can find a window within seconds and cover in-play, as a service or inside your platform.

Your data stays yours

Each report is about your prices only and is never shared. Stake data, if you share it, can stay in your own environment.
