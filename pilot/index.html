A four-week pilot, shaped around your desk.

Alerts from day one, a Ledger every week and a clear answer at the end. Nothing to install.

How it runs

Four weeks on your own prices.

Short enough to decide quickly, long enough to see the pattern: which brands, sports and hours leave prices behind, and how much of it your desk can close.

  1. Before the start

    Shaped with your traders.

    We agree the brands, markets, sports and the levels that count as a window, and set up the console the way your desk works.

  2. Day one

    Alerts go live.

    Sulku watches your published prices against the fair price and alerts the trader on shift while a window is open.

  3. Every week

    The Ledger.

    Every window, where it came from, how long it stayed open and how much of it your desk closed.

  4. After four weeks

    A clear answer.

    A review on your own figures: what Sulku found, what your desk closed, and what to do next.

What you need

Less than you would think.

The pilot runs on the prices your customers already see, so there is no integration project before the first alert.

No integration

We read your published prices per brand and market. No access to your systems.

A place to look

The Sulku console, or Slack or Teams if your traders would rather have the alert there.

Someone who owns the price

A trader or head of trading to set the levels with us and tell us what is useful.
