How it works

Fair price first, then your price

Many odds tools compare you with other books. Sulku compares you with the fair price, so it still sees the window when the whole market is behind.

    The sharp market's price with its margin removed, kept current for every match-winner and 1X2 selection you offer.

Beyond the window

What it can extend to

The fair price is the core. Once it runs next to your prices, the same comparison answers other questions your trading and risk teams ask.

  • Reaction time in-play

    How many seconds your in-play price would stay behind after a goal or a red card, and how long the market stays suspended, match by match.

  • Which accounts take the stale prices

    With stake data, each window can be linked to the bets placed into it, so risk sees which accounts find your slow prices first.

  • Odds boosts

    How far each boosted price sits above the fair price, and what the boosts cost against what they were meant to cost.

  • New markets and brands

    One engine with markets as configuration. A new market means choosing the books, leagues, currency and thresholds; the method stays the same.

How we count

Questions traders ask first

Which fair price do you use?

Pinnacle's, with its margin removed by the power method. A simple proportional split overstates the chance of outsiders; the power method doesn't, so long odds don't inflate the numbers.

Alerts would use the price at that moment. The report also checks every window against the closing price, the market's final and most informed view of the chance.

How is this different from an odds screen?

A screen shows where you are highest against other books. When books share an engine they are often wrong together: in the Finnish sample, between 43 % and 94 % of one site's windows were open at the same moment on another site using the same engine. A comparison with other books shows nothing there. A comparison with the fair price does.

Isn't most overpricing just news we couldn't have known?

Most of it, yes. Line-ups, injuries and goalies move the market after your price is set. In our sample about three windows in ten were visible at the time. Those are the ones we count, because they are the ones a value bettor uses and the ones your desk can close.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. We read the prices your customers see. If you share stake data, the report would turn estimated value into exact figures, but it isn't needed to start. Sulku could also run inside your own platform on your own feeds.

What counts as a window?

In the sample: a price at least 2 % above the sharp market's fair price at that moment, and still at least 2 % above the fair price at kick-off. For your desk, the thresholds would be tuned per sport and price band.

Which competitions?

Match-winner and 1X2 markets the sharp market prices: football, ice hockey, basketball, tennis, American football and more, local leagues included.

Will you show our numbers to anyone else?

No. Each report is for one operator about its own prices. Public figures on this page are ranges across six sites, with no site named.
