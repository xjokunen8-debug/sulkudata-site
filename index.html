How we count

Questions traders ask first

Which fair price do you use?

Pinnacle's, with its margin removed by the power method. A simple proportional split overstates the chance of outsiders; the power method doesn't, so long odds don't inflate the numbers.

Alerts use the price at that moment. The Monday report also checks every window against the closing price, the best estimate of the true chance there is.

Isn't most overpricing just news we couldn't have known?

Most of it, yes. Line-ups, injuries and goalies move the market after your price is set. In our sample about three windows in ten were visible at the time. Those are the ones we bill, because they are the ones a value bettor uses and the ones your desk can close.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. We read the prices your Finnish customers see. If you share stake data, the report turns estimated value into exact figures, but it isn't needed to start. If you would rather run Sulku inside your own platform on your own feeds, that works too.

What counts as a window?

A price that stays above the sharp market's fair price after the market has moved, for long enough that a bettor can take it. Thresholds are set per sport and price band, and tuned with your desk.

Which competitions?

Match-winner and 1X2 markets the sharp market prices: football, ice hockey, basketball, tennis, American football and more, Veikkausliiga included.

Does it work outside Finland?

Yes. The same detection runs in any market with a sharp reference price, so adding a market or a brand is configuration, not a new project. Finland is where we started.

Will you show our numbers to anyone else?

No. Each report is for one operator about its own prices. Public figures on this page are ranges across six sites, with no site named.
