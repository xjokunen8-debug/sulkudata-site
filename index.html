The product

Built for the trading desk, open all shift.

A live board of every price that is behind, a history your traders can check, and a weekly Ledger for the head of trading. Alerts can reach the trader on shift while the window is still open.

An alert while the window is still open.

One sentence to the trader on shift: the match, your price, the fair price and how long it has been open. Sulku can send it to Slack or Teams, or straight into your own trading tools, shaped with your desk.

How alerts work

Every window, and what closed it.

History keeps each window with its prices, how long it stayed open and what closed it: your own price change, the market coming back, or kick-off. Every line can be checked against your own logs.
