Privacy

What this website collects, and what we do with it. In short: very little, and nothing for advertising.

Who we are

Sulku, Tampere, Finland. Contact: jami@sulkudata.com.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers record technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Booking a demo

Demo bookings are made through Google Calendar. When you book, the name, email address and any notes you enter are shared with us so we can hold the meeting. Google's own privacy policy applies to the booking page.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and contact details to reply and to follow up on what you asked about. We do not sell or share them, and we delete them on request.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by writing to jami@sulkudata.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland.
