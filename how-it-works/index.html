Fair price first, then your price.

How Sulku decides that a price is behind, and why it compares you with the sharp market rather than with other books.

The method in four steps

From the sharp market to the bill.

A window is a price of yours that stays above the fair price after the sharp market has moved. Sulku finds it, follows it until it closes, and checks it against the fair price at kick-off.

  1. The fair price

    The sharp market, margin removed.

    The sharpest books' prices with their margin taken out, kept current for every selection we follow.

  2. Your price against it

    What your customers see.

    Read per brand and per market, and compared with the fair price at the same moment.

  3. The window

    Above fair, after the market moved.

    Opened when your price stays above the fair price long enough to matter, closed when it falls back in line.

  4. The bill

    Checked at kick-off.

    Every window is measured against the fair price at kick-off, the market's final and best-informed view.

One real window

Capitals – Rangers, 72 minutes behind.

One sportsbook in our sample, 9 October 2026. Its price solid, the fair price dashed, the open window hatched in red. Times in UTC.

  1. 17:46

    The market moves. The price does not.

    The sharp market shortens the Rangers: their fair price drops from 3.14 to 2.98. The sportsbook still has them at 3.15.

    Price 3.15Fair 2.98
  2. 17:50

    A small move, not enough.

    The price is trimmed to 3.10, still about 4 % above fair, and stays there for over an hour.

    Price 3.10Fair 2.98 → 3.00
  3. 18:58

    Fixed, 72 minutes later.

    The price moves to 3.00, in line with the fair price. With an alert, the trader on shift could have seen it at 17:46.

    Price 3.00Fair 3.00
  4. 23:15

    Puck drop. The bill.

    The fair price at puck drop is 2.95. Bets at 3.15 carried a 6.9 % edge against the book, bets at 3.10 a 5.2 % edge.

    Edge 6.9 %

Why not an odds screen

When the whole market is late, other books can't tell you.

Sportsbooks that share a supplier or follow the same feed can be late together. A screen that compares you with them shows nothing. The fair price still shows the window.

Questions

What trading teams ask first.

Which fair price do you use?

The sharp market's price with its margin removed. Sulku follows it as it moves, so a window opens against the fair price at that moment, not against yesterday's.

Each window is also checked against the fair price at kick-off, the market's final and best-informed view of the chance.

Isn't most of this just news you couldn't have known?

Much of it, yes. Line-ups, injuries and goalies move the market after your price is set. Sulku counts the windows that stayed open after the sharp market had already moved: the ones a value bettor uses, and the ones your desk can close.

What counts as a window?

A price of yours above the fair price after the sharp market moved, for long enough to act on, and still above the fair price at kick-off. The levels are set with your traders, per sport and price band.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. Sulku starts from the prices your customers see. With your own price feed it can work in seconds and cover in-play, as a service or inside your platform.

Which sports and markets?

Today the match result in football, ice hockey, basketball, tennis, American football and more, local leagues included where the sharp market prices them. More bet types and competitions can be added for your desk.

Does Sulku change our prices?

No. Sulku shows your traders which prices are behind; your traders decide what to do. Correcting prices automatically, within limits your traders set, could come later on your own feed.

Will you show our numbers to anyone else?

No. Everything Sulku finds on your prices is for you only. Public figures on this site are totals across many sportsbooks, with none of them named.
