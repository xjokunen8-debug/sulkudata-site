An independent check next to your pricing.

Sulku is an independent company from Tampere, Finland. We build one thing: an exact, honest measure of where a sportsbook's prices are behind the market.

Founder

Jami Jokinen

Jami used to be a value bettor. He bet into exactly these windows: a sportsbook's price left above the sharp market after the market had moved. From that side of the counter, it was the edge. From yours, it is money that closer watching keeps in the book.

He built the price collection behind Sulku, over 460 million prices read from 59 bookmakers and the sharp market, and now turns it into a tool for the people who set the prices.

How we work

Three rules we keep.

They shape the product as much as the code does.

Independent

No sportsbook or supplier owns us, and we sell no one's data. What we find on your prices is for you only.

Honest numbers

Every window has its times and prices, so your traders can check it against their own logs. Examples are labelled as examples.

Your prices, your call

Sulku never touches your prices. It shows your traders where they are behind; your traders decide what to do.
