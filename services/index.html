In-play

In-play, on your own feed.

In-play prices move with every goal, card and break in play. Public odds feeds are too slow to judge them, so in-play is built on your own price stream, against a sharp live price. On it, Sulku can show three things.

Behind after a goal

How many seconds your price stays behind the sharp market after a goal or a red card, match by match.

Suspensions

How long your markets stay suspended, and whether they reopen at the right price.

Live windows

The same windows as pre-match, found in seconds while the match is on, with an alert to the trader on shift.

What else we can build

The fair price is the core. The rest follows.

Once Sulku runs next to your prices, the same comparison answers other questions your trading and risk teams ask. We build them with your desk, one at a time, in the order you need them.

  • Every bet type and league

    Totals, handicaps and the leagues you price, wherever the sharp market prices them too.

  • Alerts and an API

    Alerts to Slack or Teams, webhooks, or an API straight into your trading tools.

  • Automatic correction

    Prices moved back in line within limits your traders set, with a log of every move.

  • Which accounts take stale prices

    With your stake data, each window linked to the bets placed into it, in your own environment.

  • Odds boosts

    How far each boosted price sits above the fair price, and what the boosts actually cost.

  • Your other brands and markets

    One engine, with markets as configuration: the books, leagues, currency and levels for each.

  • A console shaped to your desk

    The views, columns and levels your traders want, set up before the start and changed as you go.
